# CNC Mill designed and created by William Carmona
This repository includes various sections that point to different aspects of the CNC Mill. The CNC Mill was designed and assembled during the summer of 2026 as a personal project meant to gain experience in Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering.

CAD files can be found uploaded on GrabCAD: https://grabcad.com/library/cnc-mill-by-william-carmona-1
Timelapse of the Assembly can be found uploaded as an unlisted video on YouTube: https://youtu.be/5mN_64xgKeQ

## Analysis
Shows pdf files of calculations and equations regarding different issues related to the CNC Mill, including: gantry deflection, electrical load analysis, torque margins, and axis resolutions.

## Electrical
Png, drawio, and svg file of the electrical system. On draw.io, one can view an interactive model of the electrical system, where different layers can be turned off for a clearer view.

## Media
Videos of the CNC Mill in action, as well as the timelapse video linked above. The videos include a homing video, and two cutting videos.

## BOM
Bill of materials with prices. This list includes all mechanical and electrical components, but also tools required to fabricate and assemble the mill. Included a png and csv of the list.

## Report
Full technical report outlining the creative and design process for every large subassembly of the CNC Mill, the full electrical system, and the control system. Also includes longer mathematical foundation for calculations seen in analysis. A more descriptive fabrication process is given for the assembly and manufacturing of the CNC mill. Finally, some lessons from a previous iteration and a path moving forward are shown to illustrate future goals.
